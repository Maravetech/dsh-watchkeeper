# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this project:

- Being direct and kind in reviews and issues.
- Giving and accepting specific technical feedback.
- Owning mistakes and correcting them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may warn, remove content, or ban contributors, in proportion to the behaviour.
